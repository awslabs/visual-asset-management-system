# Evaluation fixtures — provenance

Every input file the corpus (`../corpus.yaml`) names. Open CASCADE converts every file to millimetres on import; "Declared units" is what the file itself declares. SHA-256 digests are in `SHA256SUMS.txt`; `../tests/test_cadstep_eval_corpus.py` checks them.

Only two licences are allowed in this directory: the Adafruit_CAD_Parts MIT licence (`LICENSE-adafruit-MIT.txt`, kept beside the files it covers) and U.S. Government public domain (the NIST files; the archive's `README.txt` is kept as `README-NIST-PMI-STEP-Files.txt`). A file under any other licence is fetched, not committed.

## Sources

-   **NIST MBE PMI Validation and Conformance Testing project**, archive `NIST-PMI-STEP-Files.zip` (13 976 599 bytes), <https://www.nist.gov/system/files/documents/noindex/2024/06/19/NIST-PMI-STEP-Files.zip>, linked from <https://www.nist.gov/ctl/smart-connected-systems-division/smart-connected-manufacturing-systems-group/mbe-pmi-0>. Work of the U.S. Government, public domain (17 U.S.C. §105). The archive's README states that the files were generated by different CAD software and that the information naming that software has been removed, so their `originating_system` field is empty.
-   **Adafruit_CAD_Parts** (GitHub `adafruit/Adafruit_CAD_Parts`, branch `main`), MIT licence, © 2016 Adafruit Industries. Designed in Autodesk Fusion 360. File URLs have the form `https://raw.githubusercontent.com/adafruit/Adafruit_CAD_Parts/main/<folder>/<file>.step`.
-   **VAMS** — `plate-40x40x10.step` is generated by `make_plate_fixture.py` in this directory (Apache-2.0, as the repository).

## What each file exercises

-   **plate** — the plain 40 × 40 × 10 mm plate of the M1, M6 and M7 cases.
-   **X1** — a Fusion 360 part: 88 faces, 59 × 20 × 47 mm, 35 991.9 mm³, four blind 5 mm holes along Y.
-   **X2** — an AP242 file with PMI: 117 faces, 800 × 450 × 150 mm, 14 644 823 mm³, R50 outline corners.
-   **X3** — an inch-unit flat sheet (7.5 × 0.1196 × 11 in, imported as 190.5 × 3.038 × 279.4 mm), 30 holes, thin along Y.
-   **X4** — a three-body assembly: an 82.8 × 28.8 × 90.5 mm shell (43 096.1 mm³) and two 1.5 mm end plates (3 511.5 mm³ each).
-   **X5** — a legacy AP203 thin disc, 63 mm × 3 mm with a 32 mm opening and two R1.5 toroidal edge rounds.
-   **X6** — a dense B-rep: 299 faces, 72 R0.25 tooth fillets, a 5 mm bore along Y.
-   **X7** — the X3 sheet as a 5.3 MB AP242 edition-3 export: transfer and parse time.
-   **X9** — the X3 sheet as a 6.1 MB AP242 edition-1 export with four root shapes (an empty compound first, the solid, a 5-face PMI compound and 290 annotation curve edges): the multi-root inspection and the output sanitiser.
